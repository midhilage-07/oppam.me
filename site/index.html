Your week, your way

A few good questions.

Do I need to upload something?

Yes. Open any challenge, do the activity in whatever way suits you, then upload a photo, screenshot or PDF of it. The challenge is marked complete as soon as your upload finishes. Photos can be up to 15 MB and PDFs up to 5 MB, with up to three files per challenge. Only you and the organisers can see your files. Avoid uploading private health information or other people’s photos without their permission.

How do I redeem my reward?

After 70 points, unlock and scratch your card, then download it or take a screenshot. Send it to your campaign organiser through your usual contact channel. They will verify the claim code and arrange the reward manually.

Can I participate remotely or on a night shift?

Yes. Complete the activities in any order and choose an equivalent activity that works for you. Healthcare benefits are separate from challenge points; partner booking details are still to be confirmed.

Who can see my progress?

Your name and total points appear on the leaderboard for colleagues. Your uploads, prize and claim code are visible only to you and the campaign organisers.
